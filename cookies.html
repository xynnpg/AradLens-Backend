1. What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Local storage and session storage are browser features that store key-value data without sending it automatically with every request. We use them only to operate the sign-in session, remember interface preferences and protect the service.

4. Third-party requests

The map is displayed with Leaflet, which is served from this site. Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap tile servers. When your browser loads a tile, OpenStreetMap receives your IP address, user agent and the requested tile coordinates. We do not control OpenStreetMap’s servers or its privacy practices. Please see the OpenStreetMap privacy policy for details.

We do not load Google Fonts, unpkg or other third-party font/script CDNs in the production interface. Fonts and Leaflet are self-hosted from this domain.

5. How to control cookies and storage

You can control or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings. The following links provide instructions for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Safari

If you block the strictly necessary cookies, you may not be able to sign in. Clearing local storage resets your theme, language and sidebar preferences.

6. Changes

We may update this notice when our storage practices change. The version and effective date are shown above.

No consent banner. Because we use only strictly necessary cookies and preference storage, no consent banner is displayed. If analytics, advertising or other non-essential technologies are added, we will update this notice and implement a compliant consent mechanism before they are used.
