4. Images and location data

Photos and precise coordinates can identify people, homes, vehicles and activities. We treat them as personal data and apply the following rules:

  • Uploader responsibility. The person uploading a photo must have a lawful basis for doing so. Images of identifiable people require their consent unless another lawful basis applies (for example, a public-interest task with appropriate safeguards). Images of minors, private interiors, identity documents and licence plates should not be uploaded.
  • Purpose limitation. Images are used to illustrate public locations in the guide. They are not used for facial recognition, surveillance, profiling or automated decision-making.
  • Minimisation. Upload only the image needed to describe the place. Avoid people, vehicles, house numbers and other unnecessary identifiers. Our upload flow is designed to strip location metadata from images before storage where technically supported.
  • Rights of people shown. Where a person shown in a photo asks for removal or restriction, we will assess the request and act where required by law.
  • Precise geolocation. Coordinates are used to place the location on the map. The workspace requests device geolocation only when you press the location button and your browser grants permission. You can deny or revoke permission in your browser settings.
Do not upload images of identifiable people without consent, images of children, private homes, identity documents, bank cards, licence plates, or information that could endanger a person. We may remove such content without notice.

8. Your rights

Where GDPR applies, individuals have the rights set out in Chapter III of the GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, data portability and the right to withdraw consent. These rights may be exercised through the operator’s published channel once it is available. Nothing in this notice limits rights that cannot be limited by law.

9. Cookies and local storage

We use only strictly necessary cookies and local storage to keep you signed in, remember your active account and theme, and protect the service. We do not use advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking cookies. See the Cookie Notice for the full list and how to control them.

10. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including TLS in production, HttpOnly/SameSite cookies, access controls, least-privilege administration, input validation, rate limiting, security headers, logging and backup procedures. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If we become aware of a personal-data breach that is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the supervisory authority and affected individuals as required by Articles 33–34 GDPR.

11. Children

Administrator accounts are intended for people aged 18 or over. Capture pages are intended for people aged 16 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below these ages. If we become aware that a child has provided personal data, we will delete it.

12. Automated decisions

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not carry out profiling for marketing. Basic security rules (such as rate limiting) may temporarily block abusive requests but do not make decisions about individuals’ legal status or access to essential services.

13. Changes

We may update this notice to reflect changes in the service, the law or our practices. The version and effective date are shown at the top. Material changes will be notified in the workspace before they take effect. Where the change relies on consent, we will ask for it again.

14. Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with the Romanian supervisory authority for data protection (ANSPDCP) or with the supervisory authority in your EU country of habitual residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement.
