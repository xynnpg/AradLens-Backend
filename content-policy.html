Content Policy

This policy explains what may be published through AradLens, how to report unlawful or harmful content, and how the operator handles notices. It applies to location records, descriptions, photos and any other material submitted through the workspace or a capture link.

1.0
Effective date

1. What may be published

Content must relate to a genuine public location in or around Arad and be useful to the guide. It must be accurate, respectful and lawful. You must have the rights needed to publish it.

2. Prohibited content

The following content must not be uploaded or published:

  • unlawful content, incitement to violence or hatred, harassment, threats, defamation or discrimination;
  • child sexual abuse material, sexual content involving minors, or any content that sexualises minors;
  • non-consensual intimate images or intimate images of identifiable people;
  • images of identifiable people without a lawful basis or consent, including close-ups of faces, children, private individuals in private settings, identity documents, bank cards, licence plates or medical information;
  • private interiors, private homes, security-sensitive locations, critical-infrastructure details or information that could endanger a person;
  • copyright, database, trade-mark or other intellectual-property infringement;
  • false, misleading or manipulated information about a place, including fake accessibility, safety or ownership claims;
  • spam, advertising, malware, phishing, tracking pixels or links to harmful sites;
  • personal data of third parties that is not necessary for describing a public location;
  • content that violates applicable Romanian or EU law, including consumer-protection, data-protection, advertising and content-moderation rules.

3. Images and privacy

Before uploading a photo, ask yourself:

  1. Does it show identifiable people? If yes, do I have their consent or another lawful basis?
  2. Does it show a private home, interior, vehicle plate, document or screen?
  3. Is the photo necessary to describe the public location, or can I crop or blur unnecessary details?
  4. Do I own the copyright, or do I have permission to publish it?

If you are unsure, do not upload. The operator may crop, blur, remove or refuse content that creates a privacy or legal risk.

4. How to report content

Use the operator’s published reporting channel once it is available. Please include:

  • your name and, when a reporting channel is available, a way to reply;
  • the URL or location name of the content;
  • a description of the problem and why you believe the content is unlawful or harmful;
  • for privacy reports: which person is affected and why the content is not lawful;
  • for copyright reports: the work, your ownership or authority, and the allegedly infringing material;
  • any supporting evidence you can share safely (do not send identity documents unless asked).

If you are reporting on behalf of someone else, confirm that you are authorised to act for them. Knowingly false or abusive reports may be refused.

5. What happens after a report

  1. Acknowledgement. We aim to acknowledge complete reports within 3 working days.
  2. Assessment. We review the content, the legal issue and any response from the uploader. We may request more information.
  3. Action. Where a report is well-founded, we may remove, hide, blur, restrict or annotate the content, or suspend the responsible account. Urgent privacy, child-safety or security risks may be actioned immediately without prior notice.
  4. Outcome. We inform the reporter and, where appropriate, the uploader of the outcome, subject to privacy and legal constraints.
  5. Record. We keep a record of reports, actions and reasons for accountability and to handle repeat-infringer issues.
Urgent risk. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the emergency services (112 in Romania) first. Do not wait for a content report.

6. Copyright complaints

If you believe content infringes your copyright or related rights, send a notice through the published reporting channel identifying the work, the infringing material, your ownership or authority, and a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is unauthorised. We may remove or disable access to the material and notify the uploader. The uploader may send a counter-notice with evidence of authorisation or a legal exception. We may restore content where appropriate and will respect applicable exceptions and limitations, including quotation, criticism, review and news reporting.

We maintain a repeat-infringer policy and may suspend or terminate accounts that repeatedly upload infringing material.

7. Appeals and restoration

If your content was removed and you believe the decision was wrong, reply to the removal notice with your reasons and any supporting evidence. We will review the decision and may restore the content if the legal issue is resolved or the report was mistaken.

8. Authority requests

We respond to valid requests from courts, prosecutors, data-protection authorities, consumer authorities and other competent bodies in accordance with applicable law. Where permitted, we notify the affected user. We may preserve content and records where we receive a valid preservation request or where necessary for legal claims.

9. Transparency

Where the operator is a public body or the service reaches significant scale, the operator may publish aggregate information about reports received, actions taken and response times, in line with transparency obligations and without identifying reporters or affected individuals.

10. Operator

This guide is operated by AradLens. Public operator details and the reporting channel will be published before the service is opened to the public.
